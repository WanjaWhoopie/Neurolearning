# Neurolearning
Bridging my Neuro knowledge for NeuroAI work

## Books

- **[Cortex to Classifier](https://wanjawhoopie.github.io/Neurolearning/eeg/)** (`eeg/`): an interactive EEG laboratory in ten stations. Fire a synapse, drag a dipole under the skull, fit an electrode cap on a 3D head, turn the brain-state dial, drill on artifacts, unmix a recording with ICA, average an ERP out of noise, and sit the exam.

Each book is one self-contained `index.html`. All signals are simulated for teaching; nothing here is a real recording.
